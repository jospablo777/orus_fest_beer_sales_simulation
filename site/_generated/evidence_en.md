We saw more than 40 minutes in the exchange line between 18:00 and 20:00; the simulation estimates 47 (between 31 and 72), with about 52 people in the ticket line and 86 in the exchange lines.

## How this was done {#como-se-hizo}

```{=html}
<p class="lead-claim"><strong>A computer simulation reproduces the 2026 night and tests each change on the same nights.</strong></p>
```

A computer model moves 2,000 attendees through the hall from 13:00 to 03:00 on the official running order. Each person arrives, watches their bands, gets thirsty, picks a line and gives up after waiting too long. What nobody measured (how much people drink, how long serving takes) was tried in 8,960 simulated nights, and the ones that reproduce what we saw in 2026 were kept: a relatively quick purchase at 15:30, more than 40 minutes in the exchange line between 18:00 and 20:00 (and no more than 70, the limit we assumed), long lines at both the ticket window and the exchange, and a lot of people giving up. Each change was evaluated on 64 of those nights. The ranges on this page cover 9 of 10 simulated nights. The details are in the technical pages. Results of 24 November 2026.
