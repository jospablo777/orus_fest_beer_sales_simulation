## How this was done

A computer model moves 2,000 attendees through the hall from 13:00 to 03:00 on the official running order. Each person arrives, watches their bands, gets thirsty, picks a line and gives up after waiting too long. What nobody measured (how much people drink, how long serving takes) was tried in 6,176 simulated nights, and the ones that reproduce what we saw in 2026 were kept: a relatively quick purchase at 15:30, more than 40 minutes in the exchange line between 18:00 and 20:00, long lines at both the ticket window and the exchange, and a lot of people giving up. Each change was evaluated on 48 of those nights. The details are in the technical pages.
